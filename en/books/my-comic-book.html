In English

A five-step formula for comic stories

At the heart of the book is an original five-step story structure. It helps connect an idea to a sequence of scenes and understand why each moment needs a purpose.

From story to comic page

The guide introduces panels, dialogue, speech bubbles, sound effects, camera angles, point of view, and page rhythm without relying on overwhelming theory.

Practice with templates

Creative prompts, planning space, comic book templates, and clean pages let readers apply the ideas directly. This is for anyone who wants to learn how to write and draw comics, not only fill blank panels.
