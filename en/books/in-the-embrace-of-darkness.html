In English

A gothic story with an autumn atmosphere

Old streets, fog, a secret garden, an ancient castle, and cold moonlight shape this supernatural romance. Greg is hiding a secret far darker than Dylan expects: he is a vampire.

Vampire romance with a dangerous pull

The closer Dylan gets to Greg’s world, the harder it becomes to return to her own. Their attraction unfolds alongside magic and a reality that no longer follows ordinary rules.

For readers who enjoy

This book may appeal to readers looking for gothic vampire romance, dark supernatural romance, atmospheric vampire fiction, or a Halloween-season story with an autumn setting. It is the first book in the I Am a Vampire trilogy.
