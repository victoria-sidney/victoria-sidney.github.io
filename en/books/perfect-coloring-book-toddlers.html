Simple pictures for little hands

The book contains 107 illustrations arranged from very simple to slightly more detailed. Bold outlines help young children practice staying within the lines.

Coloring and early vocabulary

Everyday objects, animals, fruits, vegetables, plants, and household items give children opportunities to recognize pictures and learn new words.

Ages and skills

Designed for toddlers ages 2–5, the pages offer a simple activity for practicing coloring, image recognition, vocabulary, and hand-eye coordination.
