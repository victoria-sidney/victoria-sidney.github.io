In English

What the guide covers

Readers will find an overview of two main reasons excess weight appears, help defining a goal, and ideas for choosing foods and habits that support a personal plan.

Diet overview and practical tools

The book reviews 17 popular diets and their potential benefits and drawbacks. It also includes simple meal ideas, recipes, light exercises, a meditation, and suggestions for maintaining a chosen weight.

For readers seeking a clear starting point

This guide is for people looking for a practical weight-loss book, a personal diet plan framework, and a straightforward overview of common approaches. It does not promise a specific result or replace individualized medical advice.
