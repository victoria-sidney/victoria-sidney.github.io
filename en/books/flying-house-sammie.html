In English · For adults

A summer you thought was gone forever

The kind of summer when days felt endless, apples ripened in the garden, and the smallest things held the greatest magic.

Old photographs can preserve a face. But they can’t preserve the sound of someone’s laughter.

A forgotten attic. A tiny house. A way into the past.

As the last days of summer slip away, Sammie discovers a faded family photograph. In it, a little girl is holding a tiny handmade house.

That girl is her great-grandmother, Isolda.

And the very same house is still waiting in the attic.

Except there’s something extraordinary about it.

The house can fly. The wind can speak. And an old apple tree remembers stories no photograph could ever tell.

What begins as an impossible adventure becomes something far more personal: a journey into family history, forgotten moments, and the connections that survive across generations.

A cozy illustrated story for adults about family memories

An enchanting, beautifully illustrated story for grown-ups about childhood wonder, family memories, and the places we carry with us long after we’ve left them behind.

For anyone who remembers summers that seemed to last forever.

For anyone who has ever held an old family photograph and wished they could step inside it.

And for anyone who has ever wanted just one more afternoon with someone from the past.

Some summers end. Others stay with us for a lifetime.

A gift for someone who keeps the family stories

For the person who saves old photographs, remembers childhood summers, or carries a faraway home in their heart, this illustrated story offers a quiet journey into family memories and belonging.

Because some places never really leave us. We carry them home in our hearts.
