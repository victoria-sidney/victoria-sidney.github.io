A journey through magical worlds

Sammie travels among clouds, meets talking birds and pink unicorns, and discovers that the flying house can make one journey through time.

A family memory from the past

Sammie chooses to travel back in time, where she meets her great-grandmother in an apple orchard and discovers the story behind the magical houses. She records the adventure in her diary.

For readers of magical stories

This book may appeal to readers who enjoy illustrated fantasy adventures, time-travel stories, magical houses, talking animals, and imaginative journeys shared by children and adults.
