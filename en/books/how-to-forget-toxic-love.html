Understand the cycle

A message can create hope, and hope can turn into idealized memories. This book helps readers separate facts from fantasy and notice the emotional, sensory, and digital triggers that restart the cycle.

Practical steps back to yourself

Exercises and worksheets help readers stop checking and chasing, set boundaries, replace automatic reactions, and rebuild routines, confidence, and independence.

For readers working through a breakup

This is a practical breakup recovery book for readers looking for ways to get over an ex, let go of toxic love, and decide what to do when an ex texts again.
