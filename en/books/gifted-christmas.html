In English

One act of kindness changes everything

Peter is a quiet, kind boy who is constantly bullied at school. Daisy is confident, popular, and the last person anyone expects to step into his world. But one Christmas, she does. Their unexpected friendship changes the direction of both their lives.

Then the Christmas magic begins: chocolate cookies that rise into the air and grant wishes, letters to Santa, and the mysterious Planet of Wishes. An ordinary winter opens into a world with rules of its own.

At its heart, this is a story about courage, kindness, and the choice to stand up for someone — one small act that can change a life.

A Christmas story to read, bake, and return to

The Christmas cookie exists beyond the story. Inside the book is an authentic family chocolate cookie recipe preserved for more than 30 years, along with templates for a magical silver Christmas sleigh and paper decorations.

Read the story, bake the cookies, and make the sleigh. After the last page, part of this world can stay in your home — on the table, on the Christmas tree, and perhaps as part of your own family tradition.

Find your way back to the magic of Christmas

We grow up, but sometimes we long to feel that Christmas anticipation again — when the world seemed bigger and magic felt possible. Open an illustrated story, bake chocolate cookies, and find your own Christmas in a cherished memory or a new tradition you create in your own home.

Give it to someone who loves the season, or keep it for your own holiday evening. The story, recipe, and paper crafts offer a way to make new memories, on your own or with people you love. Some books become part of a home. This one was made to stay.

An illustrated Christmas world to enter and keep

The book is presented as a richly illustrated cinematic graphic story. Light, atmosphere, and small visual details are part of the storytelling. Look through the available sample pages on Amazon before purchasing to see whether this visual world speaks to you.

The story, characters, plot, and text are original and written by Victoria Sidney. This is not an AI-written story.
