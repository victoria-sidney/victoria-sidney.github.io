In English

A story about friendship and kindness

Daisy and Peter come from different worlds. Their Christmas encounter becomes a story about empathy, standing up for someone, and the small choices that can change what happens next.

A Christmas book to return to

The illustrated graphic story includes a chocolate cookie recipe the author kept for more than 30 years, plus templates for paper decorations and a silver sleigh. Readers can use the book to create a family holiday tradition.

For children and nostalgic adults

The book is designed for readers who enjoy illustrated Christmas stories, magical holiday books, family traditions, and a warm return to childhood wonder.
