A kitten’s first memories

Told in the kitten’s voice, the story follows Runaway through changing seasons and small discoveries. Even in the cold, the hope of warm hands returning stays alive.

A story about comfort and belonging

Footsteps crunch through the snow, then come a welcoming embrace, a cozy home, and a full bowl. The story ends with the kitten finding the care it had been waiting for.

For readers

A gentle choice for readers looking for kitten books, illustrated animal stories, a cozy story about finding a home, or a nostalgic read that recalls childhood wonder.
