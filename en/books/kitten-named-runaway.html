In English

A small life, seen from the other side

Runaway doesn’t yet know what home means. At first, her world is Mama, the other kittens, and thick green leaves. Beyond those leaves, there simply has to be something more.

She runs away into tall grass, beneath an enormous sky and a moon hanging over the valley. She wants to see everything. Touch everything. But the wide world can be cold. And when winter arrives, nothing matters more than the hands she once feared.

An illustrated story of trust and finding home

A Kitten Named Runaway is an illustrated story for adults about fear, curiosity, trust, and a tiny life for which someone’s kindness changes everything.

Told in the kitten’s own voice, it lets you see familiar things from the other side: a bowl of food, a warm bed, a person who came back. And the gradual discovery that, with this person nearby, she no longer has to be afraid.

A gift for cat lovers who have given an animal a home

This story will especially resonate with anyone who has taken in a cat or dog, patiently earned their trust, and remembers the first time that animal slept peacefully in their home.

A thoughtful gift for a cat lover, a new adopter, or someone who knows how much patience it can take to earn an animal’s trust.

Perhaps you already know a story like this. Until now, you’ve only heard your side of it.
